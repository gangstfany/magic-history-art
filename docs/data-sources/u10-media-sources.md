## Review policy and bounded search

Each work received an institution/artist/representative identity-and-permission search and a second license-oriented or alternate-primary-source search (approximately two to five bounded query paths per work, with overlapping architecture/source searches assessed per component). Additional opens resolve candidates or policies; this is not an exhaustive proof that an open image can never be found. Searches returning unrelated works, generic article Creative Commons labels, or unavailable pages do not establish item-level permission.

Accepted release classes would be public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA with verified versions and a separately justified underlying-work permission. No fair use, noncommercial-only, no-derivatives, private permission presumed from classroom materials, CED/textbook extraction, or photographer-only clearance is used. Public museum availability is not permission. A CC photograph of modern sculpture/installation does not automatically license the artist's underlying work.

The rights ledger's `restricted` license/release classes mean **not approved under this project's release policy**. They do not necessarily mean the work or every possible image is legally copyright-restricted. Original-file and local-asset paths are null because nothing is released. Placeholder authority and ledger identity notes match exactly; public credits link to the precise CED component page without presenting that URL as a grant.

## Architecture and separate-rights caution

- AP240 exterior: [primary Spanish consolidated copyright law, Article 35(2)](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1996-8930#a35) expressly addresses works permanently situated in public places. That provision was freshly read; it was not assumed from Commons. [Ardfern's riverside photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Guggenheim_Museum,_Bilbao,_July_2010_(10).JPG) states photographic CC BY-SA 3.0; [Guggenheim-bilbao-jan05.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Guggenheim-bilbao-jan05.jpg) is another exterior candidate. However, [the Museum's primary legal notice](https://pro.guggenheim-bilbao.eus/en/legal-information) additionally asserts registered-trademark rights in the building image and prior written consent. This staged release withholds the exterior pending assessment of that separate assertion. **It does not conclude that the statutory copyright exception is invalid or that every educational identifying use infringes a trademark.** No exterior asset was downloaded, so no claimed visual candidate acceptance.
- AP240 atrium: museum architectural description confirms the interior scope, not an open grant. An exterior public-place exception was not extended to the interior.
- AP240 plan: CED explicitly credits FMGB; no eligible original diagram grant was located. Architecture is not a license to redraw a protected plan; no plan reconstruction occurred.
- AP249: architect PDF and MAXXI legal terms were checked separately for both component scopes. No blanket Italian freedom-of-panorama assumption is made. Architectural copyright and specific photography remain separately unverified.

## Sensitive and unclear views

All U10 works are modern and require underlying-work review. Extra caution is retained for AP233 (Salish/Kootenai critique), AP234 (Anmatyerr Country/cultural knowledge), AP237 (Pacific/Samoan cultural context) and AP245 (West African material and cultural references). Artist or community context is not a grant, and legal openness alone would not establish blanket cultural authorization. AP235 distinguishes Neshat from photographer Cynthia Preston; AP239 distinguishes video work from Kira Perov documentation; AP243 separates silhouettes, projected content and installation photograph; AP250 separates Ai Weiwei installation from Mike Peel photography.

The searchable CC0 lead for AP229 and photographic CC leads for AP237/AP238/AP248/AP250 were **not** treated as clearance of underlying modern works. Generic CC journal/footer labels do not transfer third-party artwork rights. Museum NC/fair-use/personal-study terms fail this project policy even if a particular classroom use might otherwise be lawful.

## Contract and verification
