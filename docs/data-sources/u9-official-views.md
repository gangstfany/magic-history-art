## Qualified creation and presentation geography

The ten navigation region IDs are `micronesia` (213, 217), `rapa-nui` (214), `hawaii` (215), `cook-islands` (216), `torres-strait` (218), `niue` (219), `new-zealand` (220), `marshall-islands` (221), `papua-new-guinea` (222), and `fiji` (223). Island/tradition-specific IDs avoid treating Micronesia or Polynesia as single undifferentiated countries. Modern country affiliations do not imply historical political borders.

The manifest states per-work geography and uncertainty. Portable works use original traditions/source communities, not current museum holdings: Hawaiian featherwork, Rarotonga, Nukuoro, Mabuiag Island, Niue, Marshall Islands and New Ireland. Unknown workshops remain unknown. AP220 uses New Zealand painting context rather than Czech birthplace, the sitter's Hokianga community, or an unsupported Auckland studio. AP223 uses Fiji presentation context without inventing a precise venue.

Five compact English learning groups, interpretive rather than College Board categories: **Monuments & Ancestors** (213, 214, 217, 220), **Rank & Sacred Regalia** (215, 216), **Ritual & Performance** (218, 222), **Cloth & Exchange** (219, 223), **Navigation & Knowledge** (221). AP217 has Polynesian cultural connections while geographically Nukuoro lies in Micronesia; map region is not a claim of one homogeneous cultural identity.

## Media and rights cautions for later asset assessment

All 15 views need separate exact-identity and rights verification before publication. The CED's source credits do not grant reuse; museum availability is not a license. Historical objects may have separate photographic rights and relevant community protocols; AP220's source explicitly notes descendant consultation for reproduction. AP216's historical engraving and AP223's archival photograph still require source-specific assessment rather than assuming age or archival access grants permission. AP222's illustrated 20th-century carving/display and modern documentary photographs require particular care. A Creative Commons photographer license alone does **not** establish permission to reproduce an underlying copyrighted artwork. This ledger flags review needs, makes no legal determination, and supplies no media assets.
