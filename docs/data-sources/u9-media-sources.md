## Policy and bounded candidate assessment

Exact object and requested component are independently assessed from photographic/reproduction permission and underlying-work rights. Queries were bounded to at most five component-focused search formulations, with candidate-page inspection and download checks; no indefinite search. NonCommercial, NoDerivatives, generic museum availability and 'no known restrictions' without an eligible grant do not satisfy this bundle's whitelist. Photographer CC alone does not clear a modern underlying carving or display. Cultural stewardship and descendant/community consultation are separate from copyright availability; no source is represented as community endorsement.

Null rows use `restricted` only as this application's conservative release-policy classification, not a legal finding that a work is copyrighted. Their credit `licenseName` reads `Open image-level rights not verified; official reference only`; the HTTPS `licenseUrl` is an exact CED identity/component page anchor, **not a license, grant, or permission request result**. Each null has zero original/local asset paths, a bilingual honest status, and an authority note identical to rights.identityNote. AP220 is deliberately withheld even though a public-domain candidate exists, because relevant cultural guidance remains unresolved.
