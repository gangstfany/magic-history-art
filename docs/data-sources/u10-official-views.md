Important distinctions:

- AP229: the artist's own chronology records initial-section presentation in Beijing in 1988 and first finished-form presentation at Tokyo Gallery in 1991. The manifest deliberately uses the former with this qualification.
- AP230: MoMA identifies European artisan fabrication for the series and a 1988 Sonnabend New York exhibition; exhibition is not fabrication.
- AP235: artist Shirin Neshat and photographer Cynthia Preston are distinct credited contributors. Iran is the political/cultural subject, not an automatically established photographic site.
- AP236: original community context is Park Street, Hartford, not Puerto Rican birthplace, later Puerto Rican museum holding, or the CED image-credit gallery.
- AP239: SCAD's own page establishes its 1996 co-commission and premiere; Savannah is original presentation, not proven filming site.
- AP245: Nsukka workshop context is distinct from Ghanaian origin. The textbook chapter discusses additional Anatsui works; those cannot substitute for the CED's exact 2003 Old Man's Cloth.
- AP248 and AP250: original specified presentation is Tate Modern's Turbine Hall. For AP250, Jingdezhen porcelain production in China is equally important context and is explicitly preserved.

## Publication rights caution

No images were copied from the CED, textbook or notes. All 32 views remain separate later asset work. A Creative Commons license from a photographer does **not** alone cover the underlying modern artwork; many example textbook captions explicitly pair CC photo credit with artist copyright. Museum collection pages, CED permission statements and educational availability do not transfer publication rights to this project. AP235 additionally separates artist and photographer authorship; AP239 requires rights review of the video work and still; AP243 includes projected artwork and silhouettes; AP240's official plan has its own source credit and cannot be copied as though it were an uncopyrighted location diagram. Copyright, applicable exceptions, jurisdiction and any artist/estate permissions require work-specific assessment. This research ledger makes no legal clearance claim and licenses no asset.
