# AP Art History Unit 6 source ledger

The frozen [Unit 6 manifest](../../data/ap-art-history-unit-6-manifest.json) controls the 14 work identities and 23 ordered view IDs. Official image-set authority: [College Board, Africa image set, printed pp. 122–128](https://secure-media.collegeboard.org/digitalServices/pdf/ap/ap-art-history-ced-content-area-6-africa.pdf), cross-referenced to the [current full CED](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap-art-history-course-and-exam-description.pdf). CED images establish scope; they are not reusable assets merely because the PDF is public.

Factual checks use the institutional/Smarthistory links below. The supplied APAH notes (PDF pp. 113–124) and Smarthistory guide volume four (AP 167–180 chapters) informed exam emphasis, not image permissions. Alternate collection examples explain traditions but do not replace the exact required object. In particular, the old guide's AP 180 transcript describes an equestrian post; the edition uses the correct enthroned king and senior wife.

Media review (2026-10-09): 23 required views, 8 open-license local WebP images and 15 reviewed restricted placeholders. The eight verified originals were downloaded only to an isolated temporary directory, optimized proportionally to a maximum edge of 2000 pixels and a maximum of 1,572,864 bytes, and visually checked. No restricted image was downloaded or copied; no private media bundle was created. The [rights audit](../../data/ap-art-history-unit-6-rights.json) and [placeholder authority](../../data/ap-art-history-unit-6-placeholder-authority.json) record exact-object evidence and source/license decisions. A placeholder means no portable open grant was verified, not that no such image exists. Open photographic licensing does not waive community protocols for sacred objects and performances.
