# AP Art History Unit 5 source ledger

All 27 required views were independently audited for exact work/view identity and image-level rights. Only Public Domain, CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA are eligible. College Board establishes identity, not permission to extract its images. Originals were downloaded to temporary storage; only optimized WebP derivatives are published.

Evidence review date: 2026-10-08. Three original fetches initially failed under network restrictions; approved access completed them on 2026-10-09. Fetch failures did not change rights classification. Every output was visually checked and preserves aspect ratio without added crop. AP157's former mask URL showed exhibition text; it is replaced with the exact Offering 20 mask photograph. AP154 now uses an explicit Commons CC BY-SA photograph instead of blanket NPS terms. AP156 and AP158 are newly open: exact aerial earthwork and original Vienna headdress; Mexico City replicas were rejected.

Cultural care: photographic copyright eligibility is not a claim of community endorsement or permission for ceremonial use. Great Serpent Mound remains a living Indigenous sacred site. For AP164, Peabody and Nuxalk masks are different objects; Quai Branly 71.1951.35.1 lacks proof of exact AP identity/state, so both required states remain restricted. AP163 CC0 is metadata only, not image rights.

Plan sequencing: U5-only live media/credit synchronization from Task 5 and ordered browser image-state fixture schema from Task 6 were pulled forward for coherence. The narrow U5 strict-loader/local-contract and private-keyset portions of Tasks 5/7 were also pulled forward after stale remote-only/count assumptions failed integration tests. No U5 study fields or other units were changed.
