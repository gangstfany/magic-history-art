# Unit 8 exact-component public-media ledger

Reviewed/accessed 2026-10-14. **21 works; 50 component slots; 38 open local WebP assets; 12 honest null placeholders.** No public hotlinks, CED/textbook image extractions, private bundles, AI reconstructions or newly invented diagrams are used.

## Authority and method

[College Board current AP Art History CED](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap-art-history-course-and-exam-description.pdf), physical pages 249-264, supplies the exact identities and required component content. The complete source was independently rendered and inspected; the frozen accounting and component descriptions are in [u8-official-views.md](u8-official-views.md). AP199 retains four photographs and two separate plans although the printed heading says five images; AP192's combined plan/elevation remains one slot.

Commons candidates were queried through its public MediaWiki imageinfo API, using file-specific extmetadata generated from the **individual file's licensing templates**, not a site footer. Selected file pages below are the canonical licensing and attribution evidence. PD historic works use the policy's PD Mark 1.0 record; this mark describes status and is not represented as a new license grant. CC0 / CC BY / CC BY-SA records use their canonical versioned URLs. Ancient works' dates are cross-checked against the CED; photography receives its own independently recorded grant. The exact Met Nataraja is a museum-donated CC0 image. AP212's modern underlying print has **no verified eligible grant** and is not published even if a hypothetical photograph might carry CC terms.

Search was bounded (normally at most five candidates per component query, with one more specific identity query where broad results were misleading). HTTP 429 prevented several small original/thumbnail requests. Failed downloads never became assets. File pages may supply licensed alternatives, but an unreadable candidate cannot prove visual identity. Unselected alternatives listed below are checked candidates, not a claim that every alternative is restricted or incorrect.

All 38 final assets were visually inspected in final contact sheets after WebP conversion. Every source frame is retained except AP210: only the other complete screen and blank inter-screen gutter are removed, retaining each **whole two-fold screen**, white at left/red at right. No internal painted region is cropped. All assets are RGB WebP, maximum edge <=2000 pixels and size <=1,572,864 bytes. Exact dimensions, conversion and source-original URLs are in the ordered rights JSON. The original source frame may already be a crop or panorama (explicitly noted). Full-scroll AP203 is necessarily very wide and short at the policy's maximum-edge scale; its separate opening detail is a genuine matching detail, not a burning-scene substitute.

A `restricted` release classification here means **not eligible for this project's verified-open release**; it does not prove legal copyright restriction. Every null record cites official authority, gives an individual reason and keeps its slot visible. The native credit schema requires a nonempty HTTPS licenseUrl; for placeholders that field points to the exact official component page, and licenseName explicitly says 'Open image-level rights not verified; official reference only'. That URL is an identity/reference link, NOT a license grant or evidence of reuse permission. No image is copied from that authority.
