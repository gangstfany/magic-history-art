### AP170 `stool-context`

The CED credits Marc Deville/Gamma-Rapho/Getty for the stool's ceremonial context. The accepted1935 primary photograph is not silently duplicated into this separate slot. Commons historical coronation/court photos can be viable only if the sacred stool and ceremonial context are both identifiable; most obvious files are marked OGL1.0 rather than an approved class, while some depict the Ga or Joint Provincial Council stool, not Asante's sacred stool. A1935-PD secondary frame remains under investigation. Source inventory https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Depictions_of_the_Golden_Stool ; primary current ruler/source https://manhyiapalacemuseum.org/otumfuo25-golden-stool-takes-centre-stage/ . Neither museum website presence nor Getty attribution is an open grant.
