## Supplemental corroboration and sensitive claims

- AP196: textbook physical p.35 / printed p.25 explicitly identifies the north mound of Hwangnam Daechong and discusses ceremonial rather than exclusively funerary use; p.37 / printed p.27 discusses burial and interregional exchange. This record is that specific crown, not any Silla crown with similar projections.
- AP197: textbook pp.40–42 / printed pp.30–32 distinguishes original bronze commission, eye-opening ceremony, later replacement and c.1203 guardians. CED's 743 and rebuilt c.1700 labels remain in identification; detailed chronological notes prevent false attribution of all surviving components to 743.
- AP199: textbook pp.47–49 / printed pp.37–39 provides Vishnu dedication and Mount Meru symbolism for Angkor Wat. [Smarthistory, Angkor Thom](https://smarthistory.org/angkor-thom/) and [Bayon: a temple with many faces](https://smarthistory.org/bayon-temple-angkor-thom/) distinguish Jayavarman VII's city and temple and discuss ambiguity of face identity. Supplemental pages were checked through current indexed search results; the Bayon full-page open returned an error. Accordingly, the wording remains explicitly qualified and does not pretend to resolve the iconographic debate.
- AP204: textbook pp.67–68 / printed pp.57–58 identifies Jingdezhen, inscription date and Daoist dedication. The objects' naming after Percival David is later collection history, not the commission's purpose.
- AP205: textbook pp.69–70 / printed pp.59–60 establishes Joseon meritorious-subject portraiture, court painting bureau, peacock rank badge and family/ancestral preservation; present Cheongwon custody does not fix the studio.
- AP206: [Getty Conservation Institute, Juanqinzhai](https://www.getty.edu/projects/alternative-climate-controls-historic-buildings/juanqinzhai-qianlong-garden-complex/) describes silk trompe-l'oeil walls and ceiling and the eighteenth-century Qianlong garden context. This corroborates an illusionistic room type, not permission to substitute any room for the exact CED photo. [World Monuments Fund, Qianlong Garden](https://www.wmf.org/projects/qianlong-garden) supplies conservation context; restoration is not treated as original construction.
- AP210: textbook pp.87–88 / printed pp.77–78 supplies screen format, compositional relationship, left white/right red distinction, tarashikomi and Rinpa's non-academic lineage.
- AP212: CED physical p.264 / printed p.257 labels an unknown artist's circa-1969 color lithograph based on Liu Chunhua. Textbook p.93 / printed p.83 introduces the earlier 1967 oil, and pp.94–95 / printed pp.84–85 provide propagation and ideology context; those cannot silently change the assessed medium. [China Pictorial, 1968 no.9, pp.12–13, archived transcription](https://chineseposters.net/resources/splendid-revolutionary-oil-painting) locates the image's narrated visit in autumn 1921. It is a contemporaneous propaganda text, used for intended narrative date, not uncritical attribution of the whole labor movement to Mao. [Chinese Posters' contextual account](https://chineseposters.net/themes/mao-anyuan) corroborates the 1967 painting's ideological planning and later reproduction. Neither supplies the anonymous CED lithograph's workshop. Notes pp.143–144 and textbook p.94's strike discussion must not compress the 1921 visit and 1922 strike into one event.

## Geography and record contract

Coordinates are approximate anchors in the application's actual 1600×800 equirectangular geography, not the Unit 2 fallback transform or claimed survey/GPS positions. A later independent polygon audit found that the initial hand-tuned coordinates calibrated to inherited lookup examples were incorrect: Japanese anchors were offshore, and several Chinese anchors fell in Vietnam or the Philippines. All 21 new U8 anchors were corrected before U9 integration; U1–7 coordinates were deliberately preserved outside this task's scope.

The real SVG confirms `x = 800 + (40/9) × longitude`, `y = 400 − (40/9) × latitude`: Egypt's 25°E/22°N boundaries occur at x911.1/y302.2, and Japan's complete polygon bounds independently agree. The independently verified primary coordinates for [Sanchi](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/524/), [Ryoan-ji](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/688/maps/), and [Longmen](https://whc.unesco.org/document/217568) give rounded anchors (1146,296), (1403,244), and (1300,246). Other points are illustrative site or qualified regional anchors, not individually GPS-source-verified survey claims. Every corrected point was numerically checked inside its corresponding country polygon.

In-situ monuments use their original sites. Portable works retain the manifest's burial findspot, regional creation context or publishing context; uncertain production cities remain uncertain. In particular AP201/203/208/210/212 do not claim newly discovered workshops. Parent integration registers these corrected coordinates directly in the world-site lookup. The regression test freezes all 21 corrected anchors in both staged and live data plus the lookup, while inherited AP1–191 hashes remain unchanged.

Non-null provenance qualifiers are copied exactly from the frozen manifest; null qualifiers are omitted. English title, stable ID, region, site name and navigation group also match the manifest exactly. Recognition anchors number 2–4 per work; each work has four bilingual keywords and one specific comparison to another actual U8 AP work. No `images` field is embedded. Media worker evidence governs exact asset status and image credits; this content task does not approve media.
