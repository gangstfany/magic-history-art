U3 has five groups: Late Antique & Byzantine; Medieval Europe; Islamic Art; Renaissance & Mannerism; Baroque, Rococo & Colonial Art. U4 has six historical families. Other Units use broad contextual periods or traditions, retaining a single group when finer chronological subdivision would be misleading.

Cross-period complexes are grouped by their core context; full dates remain in the artwork.
